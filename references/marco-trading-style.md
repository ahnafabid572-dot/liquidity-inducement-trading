## 1. Marco-r philosophy — ek line-e

Liquidity mane ekta line na, ekta **zone/block**. Price oi zone-e tene ana hoy, ekta side-ke induce kora hoy, tarpor opposite side-ke trap kora hoy, tarpor price trapped side-r opposite dike chole. Trade kora hoy block theke, opposite liquidity-r dike — kono random level, kono indicator, kono prediction theke na.

**3 ta kotha sob video-te bar bar esheche:**
1. **React koro, predict koro na.** Marked level-e price ashle reaction dekhe decision.
2. **Keep it simple, trust your system.** Chart overcomplicate kora jabe na, analysis miss holeo delete kora jabe na.
3. **Block chara trade nai.** Inducement eka, sweep eka, ekta level eka — konotai trade-r karon na.

## 2. Liquidity block — ki, kivabe aka hoy

- Block holo **shaded rectangle/zone**, ekta candle ba ekta wick tip na. Eta ekta cluster — koyekta near-equal high/low, ba ekta sideways trap zone, ba sharp move-r ager consolidation/range.
- Block aka hoy jekhane toiri hoyeche, tarpor **dan dike, future-e drag/extend** kora hoy. Prottek timeframe-e notun kore aka na — eki block carry kora hoy.
- Kintu sotorko thakte hobe: Daily block ar intraday block sobsomoy eki level na hote pare. Method-ta carry hoy (HTF swing mark koro, block shade koro, LTF reaction dekho), kintu prottek frame-e identical rectangle dhore neoa jabe na.
- Ekta level tokhon-i liquidity jokhon: price shetake **respect** kore, ba sweep kore trap toiri kore, ba sell-off/reaction diye communicate kore je okhane liquidity chilo. Prottek swing liquidity na — nijeke jiggesh korte hobe: “Ami ki biswas kori eta liquidity?”
- Choppy, sideways range nijei ekta liquidity-building block/zone. Range-r moddhe liquidity build hoy, kintu shei moddhe trade kora hoy na (Section 5 dekho).

**Block-r rong/label video onujayi bodlay:** pink/red high block, pale-blue low block, grey SL zone, teal/green profit zone, $$$ opposite pool, BOS, ENG LQ, IND BUYERS/SELLERS, Buyers/Sellers Trapped. Rong mukkho na — zone ar trap-ta mukkho.

## 3. Pura workflow — step by step, ei order-e

### Step 1 — HTF story age (direction only)
- Daily / Weekly / 4H diye shuru koro. Kon pool ekhono **untouched** pore ache? Kon dike price jete pare?
- Daily chart bhula jabe na — daily alone-i onek kichu dhore rakhe. Daily high/low theke line tene intraday-te ceiling/floor hishebe use koro.
- Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL) diye framing koro. PDH/PDL touch korlei fade korar signal na — ogulo framing.
- 4H-e level-ta **engineer** hoy — mane ekhane aro build-up/trap toiri hoy.

### Step 2 — Sob liquidity mark koro, age theke
- Range high/low, internal equal high/low, swing pool, internal level, PDH/PDL — sob mark koro.
- Untouched level somoy par holeo batil na — koyekdin ager level-o relevant thakte pare. Level tokhon-i batil jokhon sheita run hoye jay.
- Target-o age theke thik korte hobe — trade-e thaka obosthay target thik kora jabe na.

### Step 3 — Range check: price kothay ache?
- Price ki **ranging** (clear high/low-r moddhe bar bar ghurche) naki trending?
- Ranging hole age pura range-r **sob internal liquidity map koro**: range high, range low, internal equal high/low, swing pool.
- Range-r majhkhane je liquidity block dure upore ba niche pore ache, sheita current setup na — sheita shudhu distant watch level.
- Range-r moddhe jeta ke bole **“no man's land”**: upside liquidity ar downside liquidity-r majhkhane choppy zone. Ekhane typically trade kora hoy na, wait korte hoy price range-r upore ba niche jawa porjonto — ei wait din ba week-o lagte pare. Majhkhane trade korlei chop ar unnecessary loss.

### Step 4 — Inducement
- Ekta high neoa hole buyers induce hoy, ekta low neoa hole sellers induce hoy.
- Retracement upore uthe buyers-ke induce korte pare, tarpor move abar HTF direction-e continue kore — retracement-kei real move dhora jabe na.
- Internal level-o inducement hote pare. Sobcheye obvious durer structural level-ta shobshomoy trigger na — kache internal level-e trap age toiri hote pare. **Look left** — trap kothay hocche, reaction kotha theke ashlo?

### Step 5 — Trap (sweep)
- Opposite extreme sweep kore induced side-ke trap korte hobe. Sweep-ke face value-te pora jabe na — jiggesh koro: ke induce holo, ke trap holo?
- Ekta move jeta BOS dekhay, sheita asole Sellers Induced hote pare — mane trap, clean continuation na.
- Trap chara trade nai. Inducement eka trade na.

### Step 6 — Block formation
- Trap-r pore je zone toiri hoy, sheitai liquidity block. Block zone, entry/stop reference — kintu eka trade-r karon na.
- **Nearer-trap check:** Kache internal level jodi sweep hoye induced side-ke trap kore fele, tahole shei nearer level-i trigger hobe, durer structural level na.

### Step 7 — Pairing check (badhyotamulok)
- **Ekta block + ekta opposite-side liquidity target — duitai lagbe.** Opposite-e kono untaken pool na thakle, tap jotoi clean dekhak, sheita setup na.
- TP sobsomoy opposite liquidity-ke target kore: internal level = partial, HTF/external extreme = final.

### Step 8 — Entry (3 rokom, poristhiti onujayi)
**A. Direct tap entry (standard):**
Block-e price tap korle limit entry. Eta standard, kintu block-ta fresh, unmitigated hote hobe.

**B. Confirmation entry (shudhu jokhon direct miss ba RR kom):**
Direct entry miss hole, ba direct RR 3-r niche hole, tokhon-i confirmation. Confirmation mane jekono close na — **fresh LTF inducement + trap diye toiri howa NOTUN block**. Tarpor shei notun block-e entry.
- Kichu video-te confirmation aro strict: NY open-r pore, kichu low/high neoa hole, tarpor continuation ashle tobei entry. Prothom tap-e na, pre-open chop-e na.
- 1m-e “this is no entry” — confirmation chara rise-fall-rise pattern entry na.

**C. Stacking entry (same direction-e ditiyo entry):**
Prothom move-r pore pullback ashle, pullback eka ditiyo entry-r karon na. Pullback jodi **notun ekta block-e** ashe, ar shei block theke reaction/selloff ashe, tahole same direction-e ditiyo entry neoa jay, aro deep final target-r dike. Ditiyo entry-r SL ditiyo block/high-r upore.

### Step 9 — Stop Loss (SL)
- SL **pura block zone-r baire**, sathe choto spread allowance. Block-r majhkhane kokhono na, wick tip-eo na.
- Short hole SL block/high-r upore, long hole block/low-r niche. Advanced Gold video-te ei rule-ta “will never change” bola hoyeche.
- Kono block nai, ba stop eto boro je RR acceptable na — tahole **no trade**.
- Entry-r pore choto squeeze (price ektu ulta dike jawa) nije theke setup invalid kore na — shei karone stop sorano jabe na.

### Step 10 — Take Profit (TP) — stage-e stage-e
- Internal opposite level = **partial target**. Partial size video onujayi bodlay, pray 20–50% (Volume 2-te 40–50% bola hoyeche). Kono fixed % nai.
- External/HTF opposite extreme = **final target**.
- Kichu video-te partial target alada kore label kora chilo na — shekhane shudhu final target-ta clear chilo. Tai partial sobsomoy chart-e label thakbe, eta dhore neoa jabe na.
- Target holo age theke thik kora level — arbitrary extension na. Jei high/low ekhono neoa hoyni, sheitai target. Target area-e pouchale sheita decision point — automatic hold-forever level na.

### Step 11 — Management (trade cholakalin)
- Partial neowar pore **SL breakeven-e** sorano hoy, baki-ta final-r dike cholte deoa hoy. Breakeven sobsomoy na — kichu khetre “not all the time” bola hoyeche.
- Partial-r pore pullback asha normal — pullback trader-ke “phase” korbe na, baki position exit-r karon na.
- Rapid move-r pore boro open gain ferot dite chao na — tai stop-ke pore-r intervening high/low-r upore/niche roll kora jay.
- Prothom target run howar pore choice: partial nao, na hoy breakeven-e jao — karon ekta level run holeo price abar ulta dike fire ashte pare.
- Pura move dhore rakha normal dhore plan kora jabe na — partial realistically nite hobe, baki jotodur jay totodur.

### Step 12 — Session filter
- **London = buildup/trap context. New York = execution context.** Asia generally avoid kora hoy (flag, auto-block na). Kichu example-e Asia session-e entry chilo, kintu sheita exception hishebe bola hoyeche, niyom na.
- London open-e inducement build hoy, tarpor 9:30 stock/NY open-r move trigger context hote pare.
- Time confluence: candle-closure entry shudhu tokhon-i jokhon time confluence ekdom mele. NY session-r heart-e (video-te 8am bola hoyeche, timezone sampled frame-e legible chilo na) execution-e focus.
- **Holiday-te trade na.**

## 4. Timeframe hierarchy — live check-e eta badhyotamulok

Eta age bhul hoyechilo, tai ekhane sposhto kore lekha holo:

- **Daily + 4H = direction only.** Ekhane alert-entry LB detect kora jabe na.
- **1H = LB detection.** HTF direction → 1H inducement → opposite trap → 1H block zone.
- **15m / 5m = refinement only, cascade-e, EKI 1H block-r:**
  1. Age 15m-e try koro. 15m clean refine korte parle **15m-ei thambe, 5m-e jabe na**.
  2. 15m clean refine korte na parle tobei 5m-e jao.
  3. 15m/5m zone jodi 1H block theke na ashe, sheita kokhono setup na.
- Kono clean 1H block na thakle alert nai, 5m/15m-e choto zone thakleo na.
- Kichu video-te onno breakdown dekhano hoyeche (jemon Daily → 4H → 1H → 15m, ba 1D → 1H → 15m → 5m, ba 15m → 5m → 1m). Ogulo oi video-r example-r order. Live alert-r jonno uporer 1H-detection + 15m-tarpor-5m cascade-i kaj-r niyom — karon user ei cascade-ta thik kore diyeche.

## 5. Stay-out filter — ei obosthay trade NOY

- Induced high-te buy na.
- Low already neoa hoye gele tarpor sell na.
- Mid-range chase na.
- Sellers induce hoye trap howar pore abar buy na — tokhon valid side sell hoye jay.
- Buyers induce howar pore high-to-low sell (Lower prob) na.
- Block chara, pairing chara, HTF direction-r biruddhe, RR 3-r niche — konotai setup na.
- Reaction-only tap entry na. Approaching block manei safe na — jodi block-r beyond-e aro liquidity build hoye thake, taholeo safe na.
- Range/no-man's-land-r moddhe trade na.
- Confirmation na pele entry valid dhora jabe na.
- HTF context jodi LTF entry-ke pochondo na kore (4H-r price action-r nature bhalo na lagle), tahole LTF entry downgrade/veto hobe.

**No-more rule (sobcheye sharp form):**
Low neoa hoye gele ar sell na. High neoa hoye gele ar buy na. Karon tokhon oi side-r liquidity already neoa hoye geche, trade shuru korte hobe block theke, ekhono-untaken liquidity-r dike.

## 6. Risk:Reward (RR)

- Marco-r nijer rule: **1:3-r jonno wait koro**, prothom target-e 2.2 RR-e settle koro na — aro deep target nao.
- Live alert-e setup tokhon-i qualify korbe jokhon **final HTF opposite pool porjonto RR >= 3.0**.
- RR < 3.0 hole direct trade na — sheita shudhu CONFIRMATION_ONLY (fresh LTF block diye confirmation ashle tobei vaba jabe).
- Kono fixed RR sob video-te nai — kichu example-e RR bodleche. Tai live rule hishebe 3.0 minimum-ta dhora hoyeche, user-r niyom onujayi.

## 7. Discipline — je karone beshirbhag trader fail kore

Ei part-ta “WHY You CAN'T Trade” video theke sobcheye important:

- **Entry miss hole line delete koro na, analysis scratch koro na, chase koro na.** Original marked level ar target rekhe dao — price pore shei marked low/high-tei jete pare.
- **Predict na, react koro.** Marked level-e price ashle reaction dekhe siddhanto.
- **Keep it simple.** Chart-e line kom rakho — “One line. That's it. So, you plot a ray on there.” Proper level mark kora thakle baki-ta price-i dekhabe.
- **Patience.** High plot kore wait koro, price fire asa porjonto. Block form howar jonno wait koro, tag/fill-r jonno wait koro — move-r pichone chase koro na.
- **Catching absolute top/bottom sobsomoy hoy na.** Ekta textbook example-e top catch kora mane ei precision routinely expect kora jabe na.
- **Level-ke respect korte hobe.** Sell-off-r pore low respect korle tobei sheita entry context. Respect chara level meaningless.
- **Dekhte kemon lagche, sheita diye bichar na.** Plan dekhte falling knife dhorar moto lagte pare, kintu pre-marked system onujayi cholte hobe.

## 8. Live trade-finding checklist (proti 30 minute-e, 10:00–22:00 Dhaka)

Prottek pair-r jonno, ei checklist-e sob “YES” hole tobei alert:

1. [ ] Daily/4H direction thik ache? (direction only)
2. [ ] 1H-e fresh, unmitigated block ache? (inducement → opposite trap → block)
3. [ ] Price block-e tap korche / sobematro closed candle-e tap koreche?
4. [ ] Block-ta nearest trap? Naki kache internal trap shetake replace koreche? (nearer-trap check)
5. [ ] Block paired with untaken opposite pool? (pairing check)
6. [ ] Kono stay-out filter trigger hocche na?
7. [ ] 15m refinement clean? (hole 15m-ei thambe) — na hole 5m refinement.
8. [ ] SL pura 1H block-r baire? (+ spread allowance)
9. [ ] Final HTF pool porjonto RR >= 3.0?
10. [ ] Session thik ache? (London buildup / NY execution; holiday na; Asia avoid, exception chara)

**Kono ekta “NO” hole:** Alert na. Jodi ranging hoy, tahole range ar tar internal liquidity state file-e likhe rakho, ar notun block form howa watch koro. Durer block-ke current setup bole alert koro na.

**Alert message-e thakbe:** pair, direction, 1H LB zone, refined entry zone + kon step-e thamlo (1H only / 15m / 5m), entry, SL, partial, final, RR, current price, Dhaka check time, ar sposhto note — level-gulo approximate live-chart reading, kono order place kora hoyni, user-ke MT5-e manually trade korte hobe.

**Stacking alert:** Prothom trade-r pore jodi pullback notun block-e ashe ar reaction dey, tahole stacking setup hishebe alada kore janate hobe — pullback eka stacking na, block lagbei.

## 10. Ja Marco NA — ei style-e kora jabe na

- Kono indicator-based entry, kono breakout chase, kono mid-range guess.
- Ekta line-e trade, ekta candle-e trade, ekta sweep-e trade.
- Durer block-ke force kore current setup banano.
- 5m/15m choto zone-ke standalone block banano.
- RR 3-r niche direct entry.
- HTF-r biruddhe LTF trade.

- Trade-e thaka obosthay target invent kora.
- Stop-ke block-r moddhe rakha, ba choto squeeze-e stop sorano.
